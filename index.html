Get the right help.

For app, account, purchase or privacy help, email Carecircle89@icloud.com. Include your app version/build, iPhone model and what happened. A screenshot is useful. Never send a password, payment-card details or a verification code.

Cloud AI creation is currently paused. Repeatedly tapping Create will not enable it. Local previews are on-device illustrations, not AI-generated footage. Your existing draft and original media do not need to be deleted to troubleshoot the pause.

Creation and local previews

Loop Moments combines your chosen swing video and landing reference with an illustrative flight. The original footage and reviewed ball points are distinct from generated or modeled flight. GPS/photo references do not make AI terrain a surveyed map, and the app does not measure spin or launch speed from a single video.

When cloud creation is available, review the sharing disclosure before authorizing transfer to Supabase and Higgsfield. You can choose an on-device preview instead. If a creation already has a saved server request, use its recovery/check action rather than making replacement drafts to retry a charge.

Purchases and subscriptions

Apple handles App Store payments. Use Restore purchases in Account to recover a purchase for the same Loop account. Manage or cancel renewals in iPhone Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions, or open Apple’s subscription management. Account deletion alone does not cancel a subscription.

If you were charged but cannot access the service, contact support with the app/account context—not card details. For Apple refund requests, use reportaproblem.apple.com. Prices and availability are shown by Apple before purchase; this page does not offer a purchase or promise a generation allowance.

Account and data deletion

Registered users can open More → Account & cloud → Delete account. Read the confirmation carefully: cloud data removal is separate from local drafts and subscription cancellation. If the control is unavailable, or a guest session in an older build needs deletion, email support about data deletion. We may need to verify ownership; do not share credentials.

Read the Privacy Policy for data categories, AI processing, provider retention limits and your choices.

Before sending a bug report

  1. Note the exact screen and message, app build and approximate time.
  2. Check whether the issue is local preview, account access, a saved cloud job or final export.
  3. Keep the original clip and draft. Do not uninstall just to clear an error unless you have safely exported what you need.
  4. Email support. Send private footage only if you choose to and it is necessary for the investigation.
