This policy explains how Loop Moments handles information when you use its golf-video tools. Contact Carecircle89@icloud.com for privacy questions, access, correction or deletion requests.

Current service status: new cloud AI creation is paused. Local drafting and on-device previews remain separate. This policy describes both existing account/cloud data and the processing involved when cloud creation is available; it does not promise that generation is currently enabled.

Local drafts and previews

The app stores drafts, imported videos and photos, reviewed ball points, local previews and finished exports in its app storage on your device. Local preview rendering does not require sending your footage to an AI provider. System services such as maps, account access, purchase checks and availability checks can still use the network. Files you export to Photos, Files or another app are governed by the destination you choose.

Information used for cloud features

  • Account information: email and display name if you register, authentication credentials handled by Supabase Auth, and account/session identifiers. A guest creation session can have an account identifier without an email address.
  • Selected media and audio: full source footage, a reviewed-tracer version of that footage, landing photographs and derived reference images or motion-guide video. Audio included in selected source videos can be uploaded with the video. The app does not need microphone recording to process that imported audio.
  • Shot information: course, hole, club, title, selected start/landing coordinates, reviewed ball observations and illustrative flight parameters. Imported media can contain capture or location metadata. GPS access is requested when you use the location control; manual pins are an alternative.
  • Creation and account records: processing status, media references, result files, feedback, cost/usage accounting and entitlement records. A random installation identifier supports allowance and duplicate-use controls; the server associates its hash with the relevant account/usage records.
  • Purchases: Apple processes payment. Signed transaction data is sent to our backend to verify account entitlement. Loop Moments does not receive your payment-card number.
  • Support and service logs: information you email us and operational/security logs that can include IP address, device/client details, timestamps and error/status information.

These data support the features you request, account security, recovery, billing verification and service operation. AI-generated flight, terrain and distance are illustrative estimates, not measured sports-performance data.

Where information goes

  • Supabase provides authentication, database and private file storage under our project. It processes app customer data on our behalf and instructions under its data processing addendum. Its general privacy notice covers its own website/service interactions, not that customer-data processing.
  • Higgsfield is the configured third-party AI video-generation service. When you authorize an available AI creation, the backend provides reference-media access and shot-derived instructions so Higgsfield and its service/model providers can generate a continuation. See Higgsfield’s Privacy Policy.
  • Apple provides platform services, StoreKit purchases, system media selection and maps. See Apple’s privacy information.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this static support site and may process ordinary connection/security information. These pages contain no app-upload form, advertising scripts or analytics code added by us. See GitHub’s privacy statement.

Important AI-provider limit: Higgsfield’s public policy says inputs and outputs may be used to train or improve its and its affiliates’ models. We have not verified a separate zero-retention or no-training commitment for this app’s developer-API use. Do not assume media sent for AI creation is excluded from those practices. Its policy also describes third-party model/compute providers, international processing and its age restrictions. Do not upload sensitive material or another person’s identifiable media without appropriate rights and permission.

Our optional approved-asset contribution setting is separate from permission to process a creation and does not override the AI provider’s own terms. Where that option is offered, turning it off stops new shared reuse through Loop’s contribution workflow.

Choice and sharing

You may decline cloud AI processing and use on-device editing/preview features instead. Do not start a cloud creation unless you agree to the described transfer. Withdrawing permission stops future requests; it cannot undo a request already sent. Contact support for assistance with earlier data. In-app social/Circle sharing is deferred from the upcoming version-one release; older builds or previously shared data may differ. A private cloud copy is separate from exporting a file through the system share sheet.

Loop Moments does not add an advertising identifier or an advertising SDK. We do not sell your selected footage to advertisers. Third-party services have their own policies and website tracking practices; those are not a promise of zero collection by those services.

Retention and deletion

Local files remain until you remove them from the app or remove the app’s data. Copies saved elsewhere are separate. Cloud account records, source uploads, renders and private saved reels may remain while the account exists unless removed; we do not currently promise an automatic short retention period for uploaded media.

Registered accounts can request deletion in More → Account & cloud → Delete account. If that control is unavailable—for example for a guest session in an older build—email support for help identifying and removing the associated cloud data. Do not email passwords or verification codes. We may need reasonable information to confirm the request concerns your account.

The deletion workflow removes the account’s application records and user-scoped files from our active backend and deletes the authentication account. Local drafts are not removed by cloud-account deletion. Operational backups, logs or records held by Apple and other providers can follow their own retention obligations. Deleting a Loop account does not automatically delete copies already transferred to Higgsfield or files you shared elsewhere; contact us for help with a provider request.

Higgsfield’s published policy describes a 30-day active-system period for removed content/account recovery, possible backup/legal retention and limits on removing content already incorporated into trained models. Those statements are provider terms, not a verified deletion guarantee for a particular API job.

Deleting an account does not cancel an Apple subscription. Manage or cancel it in iPhone Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions. You may request access, correction, deletion or withdrawal of consent through the support address; applicable legal rights and verification requirements apply.

Security, location and updates

Cloud connections use HTTPS, account tokens are stored in the device Keychain, and backend access controls restrict account-owned records. No system can guarantee absolute security. Service providers may process data outside your country. Cloud AI creation through Higgsfield is restricted to people aged 18 or older, or the age of majority in their jurisdiction if higher. This provider restriction concerns cloud AI creation, not a claim that local-only previews send data to Higgsfield. Eligibility requirements must be met before using that service.

We will update this page when data practices change. Material changes to sharing scope require updated disclosure before new processing. This page is a data-practice notice, not a certification that the app has passed App Review.
